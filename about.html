About Rockfort Metal Coating

Founded in 2003, Rockfort Metal Coating Sdn Bhd is a specialized mechanical engineering workshop based in Sungai Rasau Industrial Park, Klang, Selangor.

We provide end-to-end solutions focused on restoring, protecting, and extending the life of industrial components — offering a cost-effective alternative to full replacement.

Our Vision

Improving quality, increasing service life, and saving cost through reliable engineering solutions.

Our Mission

To be our customers’ one-stop solution centre with relentless determination to get the job done.

Industries We Serve

  • General engineering vendors and manufacturers
  • Power plants
  • Shipyard and marine
  • Food industry
  • Oil & gas
  • Water and waste management (including Indah Water)

With a skilled team of approximately 20 people and over 20 years of combined experience, we deliver practical repair and fabrication work for pumps, motors, impellers, shafts, and more.
