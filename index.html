Our Core Services

Thermal Spray

Restore worn components to OEM specifications or apply protective coatings. Widely used for pumps, motors, and industrial parts across multiple sectors.

Fabrication & Machining

Metal-to-metal fabrication from engineering drawings. Shafts, frames, jigs, panels, and ID fans using in-house lathe and milling capability.

Dynamic Balancing

Precision balancing up to 4,000 RPM for rotors, impellers, ID fans, and turbines. Delivers smoother operation, higher performance, and longer service life.

Industrial Coating

Rebuild and protect parts against erosion and corrosion while improving efficiency. Impeller coating, pump casings, and internal pipes.
