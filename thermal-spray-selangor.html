Thermal Spray Coating in Klang, Selangor

Metal spray and protective coating for industrial components, done at our Klang workshop.

Rockfort Metal Coating Sdn Bhd carries out thermal spray and metal spray at its workshop in Sungai Rasau Industrial Park, Klang, Selangor. Thermal spray is our metal coating method for building material back onto a damaged surface and for applying a protective layer against erosion and corrosion. We restore components to OEM specifications. Companies looking for thermal spray in Selangor, thermal spray coating in Klang, or metal spray in Malaysia can send the part here, and the machining that follows is done in the same workshop.

Parts we spray and coat

Pumps, motors, shafts, and other industrial parts are the core of the work. A typical shaft job is journal rebuilding: a heavily scored, worn journal is rebuilt by metal spray, then precision-ground on the lathe to a smooth, mirror finish. Pump impellers that have corroded or eroded can be given a protective coating. The project gallery shows an impeller refinished with a smooth protective ceramic coating. Pump casings and internal pipes are coated in the same line of work, to rebuild the surface, protect it, and improve efficiency.

When a job needs a new or modified piece, we fabricate it from an engineering drawing or a sample. That includes shafts, frames, jigs, fixtures, panels, and ID fans, with welding and machining done in-house. Suitable fabrication jobs have no minimum order quantity. How a thermal spray job is handled

  1. The component is received at the workshop at No. 11, Jalan 39/16, Off Connaught Bridge, Kawasan Perindustrian Sungai Rasau, in Klang.
  2. The damaged area is restored by metal spray, or a protective coating is applied, using a material suited to the part.
  3. The surface is machined or precision-ground in-house. Shaft journals are ground on the lathe after metal spray. A pump housing that needs a rebuild is restored by weld build-up and then precision-machined to a smooth bore.
  4. When the job needs a new or modified piece, we fabricate it in the same shop from a drawing or a sample.

Common questions

What is thermal spray, or metal spray?

Thermal spray, also called metal spray at our workshop, is a metal coating method. We use it to restore a damaged component to OEM specifications or to apply a protective layer. Machining and grinding are done in the same Klang workshop.

Which parts do you thermal spray in Klang?

We work on pumps, motors, shafts, and other industrial components. Shaft journals are rebuilt by metal spray and precision grinding. Pump impellers can receive a protective coating, and we coat pump casings and internal pipes.

What coating materials can you apply?

Carbon steel, aluminum, stainless steel, bronze, Inconel, Babbitt, nickel alloys, Hastelloy, and other materials suited to the part.

Do you machine the component after spraying?

Yes. A sprayed shaft journal is precision-ground on the lathe. We also machine shafts, frames, jigs, panels, and ID fans with the in-house lathe and milling equipment.

Is repairing a part a way to save the cost of replacement?

Yes. Restoring and protecting the existing component is a cost-effective alternative to full replacement. Our vision is improving quality, increasing service life, and saving cost through reliable engineering solutions.
