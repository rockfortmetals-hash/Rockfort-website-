Our Services

Comprehensive repair, rebuild, and fabrication solutions for industrial components.

Thermal Spray

Metal coating technology to restore damaged components to OEM specifications or apply a protective layer. Used across aviation, oil & gas, power, and general engineering.

Materials: Carbon Steel, Aluminum, Stainless Steel, Bronze, Inconel, Babbitt, Nickel Alloys, Hastelloy, and more.

Thermal spray and metal spray coating in Klang, Selangor

Dynamic Balancing

Precision balancing of rotating components up to 4,000 RPM. Improves running conditions, increases performance, and extends service life.

Components: Rotors, impellers, mechanical turbines, motor and pump shafts, ID fans.

Dynamic balancing and rotor balancing in Klang, Selangor

Fabrication & Machining

Full metal-to-metal fabrication capability. We manufacture parts from engineering drawings or samples using in-house lathe, milling, and machining equipment.

  • Shafts and rotating components
  • Frames and structural metalwork
  • Jigs, fixtures, and panels
  • ID fan fabrication
  • Other custom mechanical parts

Welding methods: MIG, TIG, ARC, Powder Fusing. No minimum order quantity for suitable jobs.

Industrial Coating

Rebuild and protect parts damaged by erosion and corrosion while improving efficiency.

Typical applications: Impeller coating, pump casings, internal pipes.
